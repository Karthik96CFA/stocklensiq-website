What StockLens IQ is

A product to help Indian market participants study listed companies and manage portfolio information in one place, using exchange and company-filing data. It is being developed in India as an independent research effort.

Equity research

Structured analysis of Indian listed companies using exchange data and company disclosures.

Portfolio analytics

Tools to review holdings, risk and exposure in a single view.

Alerts & market context

Monitoring of corporate announcements and broader market conditions.

Research focus

Our work centres on getting the data and the testing right before making any claim.

Data integrity

Careful handling of corporate actions, point-in-time data and security identity so that historical analysis is not distorted.

Rigorous validation

Strategy and scoring ideas are tested out-of-sample and independently reviewed; research is treated as exploratory until confirmed.

Transparency

We prefer documenting limitations over overstating results.
